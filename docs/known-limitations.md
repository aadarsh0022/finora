# Known limitations

- Backend identity and shared finance foundations are tested. Complete finance
  services and mobile finance workflows remain in development; MVP is unfinished.
- Atlas is connected locally; automated tests exclusively use disposable MongoDB.
- Production email needs a verified sender/key; local outbox writes real recovery
  messages to disk but does not deliver internet email.
- Legal/support pages are explicit development drafts. Operator identity, monitored
  contact, infrastructure retention policy and review remain launch gates.
- No signed AAB, native device QA, store submission or closed testing completed.
- No billing, advertising, bank integrations or AI calls. Reserved collection indexes
  do not mean budgets, goals, recurrence, reports or backup workflows are delivered.
